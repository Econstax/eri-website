Our Story

Economics isn't just a subject — it's a superpower.

Every decision you make, from what you buy to who you vote for, is shaped by economics. We're on a mission to make sure you understand the forces driving the world around you.

The Spark

It started with a radical idea: what if students didn't just memorize economic theories, but actually learned to think like economists? Founded by Viyan Patel, ERI set out to change how economics is taught, learned, and applied.

02

The Mission

We bridge the gap between textbook economics and the real world. Through competitions, seminars, and hands-on programs, students learn to analyze markets, evaluate policies, and make decisions that matter.

03

The Movement

What began as one student's vision grew into a community of learners, educators, and future economists. We're building a generation of economic thinkers ready to shape tomorrow.
